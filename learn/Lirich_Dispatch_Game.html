Dispatch

Lirich runs roughly 6 vehicles and 63 bins across 219 sites. This is one round out of that book — twelve sites, three hooklifts, one vacuum tanker, eleven bins, five days.

Four things can sink you and they are all connected. A client left waiting. No empty bin to send. The floor at 23 Gul Drive full, so nothing can be tipped. And the cash to fix any of it. Solving one by spending usually makes another worse.

Every call shows the vehicles free to take it — one tap sends one. Tipping, sorting and baling run themselves — the only decisions here are the ones a dispatcher actually makes. Marine work goes in the tanker and nothing else.

Advanced staff and visitor simulation — not part of pupil progression. No accounts, no login, no data collected. Money figures are indicative teaching numbers; the tonnages and the mass balance are Lirich’s own published ones.
