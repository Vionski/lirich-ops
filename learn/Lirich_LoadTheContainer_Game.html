Load the Container

One 20-foot container. 28 tonnes of payload, 33 cubic metres of space. You will almost never run out of both at the same time.

Only water weighs a tonne per cubic metre. Loose film is closer to 20 kg/m³. Bale it and it is 400. The container has not changed size — the material has.

The level target is the minimum useful shipment, not the container's 28-tonne maximum. Reach it before space or payload runs out. You get a limited number of bales and a limited number of refusals.
