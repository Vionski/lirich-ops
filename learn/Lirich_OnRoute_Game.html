On Route

Three lanes, three streams. Bins are already out on the kerb — some of them in the wrong place.

Collect what belongs, drive round what does not. A paper bin in the paper lane is a collection. A paper bin in the plastics lane is contamination, and picking it up is how a clean load stops being clean.

Arrow keys, on-screen buttons, or swipe. A 3–2–1 countdown gives you time to find the controls. Lane changes are announced before the next collection. Four contaminated pickups and the load is rejected.

No accounts, no login, no data collected.
