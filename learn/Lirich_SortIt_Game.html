Sort It!

Swap two items to line up three of the same. Matched items go to the right stream. Dirty items like a greasy pizza box jam the line — match next to them to clear them.

Sorting rules follow NEA's list of what is recyclable and what is not. CO₂e figures are indicative for now. No accounts and nothing personal is saved. A class code is a label only and is never sent anywhere. Your stars stay on this device.
