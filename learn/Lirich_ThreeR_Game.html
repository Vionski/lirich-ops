The 3Rs

Reduce, Reuse or Recycle?

Ten everyday moments. For each one, pick the best thing to do. The order matters:

1 · Reduce The best waste is the waste never made.
2 · Reuse Use it again before it becomes waste.
3 · Recycle Clean, in the right bin, when it is finished.

The best choice scores in full. A good choice that is lower on the ladder scores half.

What can go in the blue bin comes from NEA's list of items that are recyclable and not. Weights are for scoring only. Nothing personal is saved.
