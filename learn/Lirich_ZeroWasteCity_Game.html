Operator mission · Sec 2–3

Zero Waste City

You run the waste system of an island city of six million people. Everything that is thrown away has to go somewhere, and there is one landfill.

The real deadline. Singapore's only landfill, Semakau, is expected to run out of space by 2035 at current disposal rates. You have until 2040. Keep it open.

This is an advanced classroom simulation. Every year you choose what to fund. Build too little and the landfill fills. Build too much and you run out of money. Push people too hard and you lose public support — and support is what actually drives recycling.

No accounts, no login, no data collected.
