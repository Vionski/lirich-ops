Play safely. Learn privately.

A plain-language guide for learners, families, teachers and Lirich staff using this controlled beta.

Never touch, open or move unknown waste to copy something shown in a game. Tell an adult or trained worker. The games teach decisions; they do not teach physical waste handling.

What the beta saves

There is no learner account or login. This browser stores game progress on this device: stars, scores, kilograms, collected learning cards, streaks and the sound setting.

It does not ask for or store a learner's name, mobile number, email address, school, location or device identifier.

What leaves the device

In the board-off beta, game progress is not sent to Lirich or a shared leaderboard. There are no advertising trackers in the supplied game package.

Opening the official Lirich website follows an external link and is then subject to that website's own practices.

Your controls

  • Use “Clear my progress” on the hub to erase saved game progress.
  • Sound starts off.
  • The games respect the device's reduced-motion setting.
  • Closing a private browsing session may erase progress automatically.

Educational boundaries

Games simplify real systems to make them playable. Scores, weights, costs, emissions and operating situations marked as indicative are teaching examples, not quotations, service promises or waste-acceptance instructions.

Actual collection and disposal requirements must be confirmed with Lirich when arranging a service.

Accessibility

The beta supports phone, tablet and keyboard testing, labelled controls, visible focus and reduced motion. If something cannot be completed without a mouse, is difficult to read, or causes discomfort, record it on the feedback sheet.

Questions or concerns

Stop the activity and tell the supervising adult if a safety message seems wrong or unclear. Staff can report the exact game, level, device and what happened through the normal Lirich project contact.

For company and service enquiries, use Lirich's official website.
